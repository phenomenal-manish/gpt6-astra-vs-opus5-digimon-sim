# Media Index

Inventory of evaluation media included in this repository. All screenshots are
1280×900 PNG, captured with `chrome-devtools-axi` at the same viewport size for
both builds.

## Comparisons (`media/comparisons/`)
- `01-main-view.png`: Opening-screen comparison — Astra's nursery dashboard vs. Opus 5's handheld device shell.
- `02-evolution.png`: Digivolution-reveal comparison — Astra's full-screen HTML card vs. Opus 5's in-canvas pixel-font title card.
- `03-battle.png`: Battle-scene comparison — Astra's turn-based command duel vs. Opus 5's single-button auto-resolving fight.

## GPT-6 Astra — Digi Garden (`media/astra/`)
1. `astra-main-view.png`: Starting screen — Koromon in the nursery habitat, stats panel and evolution roadmap footer.
2. `astra-evolution-agumon.png`: Digivolution reveal — Koromon → Agumon (Rookie), "Meet your Rookie" continue button.
3. `astra-main-champion.png`: Care screen after reaching Champion — Greymon in the habitat, stats maxed out for this run.
4. `astra-battle-start.png`: Battle arena at the opening turn — Greymon vs. BlackAgumon, both at full HP, rival's next move shown.
5. `astra-battle-victory.png`: Victory screen — Greymon wins via a charged Mega Flame, session record updates to 01 W / 00 L.

## Opus 5 — Digilab (`media/opus5/`)
1. `opus5-main-view.png`: Starting screen — a Digi-Egg on the handheld's device shell, before any care actions.
2. `opus5-evolution-nubbimon.png`: The egg hatches into Nubbimon (Baby), first pixel-font title card.
3. `opus5-evolution-flash.png`: Mid-digivolution light-ray flash effect, transitioning Nubbimon → Puffmon.
4. `opus5-evolution-kobumon.png`: Digivolution reveal — Puffmon → Kobumon (Rookie); the Battle button unlocks in this same frame.
5. `opus5-champion-blazorimon.png`: Digivolution reveal — Kobumon → Blazorimon (Champion), the Power-stat branch of the three possible Champion forms.
6. `opus5-battle-start.png`: Battle scene at Rookie tier — Kobumon vs. Grubmon, both at full HP, "Grubmon blocks the path."
7. `opus5-battle-victory.png`: Battle scene after a win — Kobumon beats Frostmon, session record reads 2 wins.
8. `opus5-battle-champion-start.png`: Battle scene at Champion tier — Blazorimon vs. Skullmon, both at full HP.
9. `opus5-battle-champion-outcome.png`: End-of-session record — 3 wins, 2 losses, showing the RNG-driven battle outcomes are genuinely uncertain even at Champion tier.

## Videos (`media/video/`)
- `astra-demo.mp4`: Frame-sequence recording of a full Digi Garden playthrough — feed/train to Agumon, feed/train to Greymon, then a battle to victory (Attack, Attack, Guard, Mega Flame). 18s, ~370KB.
- `opus5-demo.mp4`: Frame-sequence recording of a full Digilab playthrough — hatching the egg, training/feeding through Nubbimon and Puffmon, digivolving to Kobumon, two Rookie-tier battles, resting, digivolving to Blazorimon, and a Champion-tier battle. 37s, ~630KB.
